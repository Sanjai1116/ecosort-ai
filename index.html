The four bins

Every item on Earth falls into one of these, based on what happens to it after you throw it away.

Wet Waste

Organic and biodegradable — food scraps, peels, garden waste. Breaks down into compost.

Dry Waste

Non-biodegradable but recyclable — plastic, paper, metal, glass. Clean before binning.

E-waste

Anything with a circuit, battery, or cord. Needs a dedicated e-waste collection point.

Hazardous

Toxic, sharp, or chemical items. Never mix with regular trash — handle separately.

How this works

You type an item's name. The assistant matches it against waste-category knowledge and reasons out which bin it belongs to, why, and how to dispose of it — the same kind of classification and information-retrieval task an AI is well suited for, without needing a trained model.
